# Anatomical brain model attribution

`brain.glb` is derived from BodyParts3D 4.0's 99% polygon-reduction IS-A OBJ archive.

- Source data: BodyParts3D, Copyright The Database Center for Life Science
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Dataset: [BodyParts3D](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/)
- Browser-ready model source: [OrganUI Brain](https://github.com/organui/brain-3d)

OrganUI selected and grouped 45 source meshes into 19 runtime structures, triangulated faces, recentered and uniformly scaled coordinates, changed axes from Z-up to Y-up, recomputed smooth normals, assigned illustrative colors, and converted the OBJ geometry to GLB. This website retains the geometry and replaces its illustrative colors with a translucent glass material at runtime.
